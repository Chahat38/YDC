About YDC

The Prime Minister’s Youth Development Center at Kohat University of Science & Technology.

Empowering youth, one student at a time

We are committed to empowering youth by providing career counseling, fostering entrepreneurship and enhancing job placement opportunities.

Through extracurricular activities, student clubs and societies, we cultivate leadership, teamwork and a positive attitude.

  • Career counseling
  • Entrepreneurial support
  • Scholarships & financial aid
  • Job hunting & networking
  • Digitalized university services
  • Extracurricular activities

Our focus: the 4 E’s

Education

Providing knowledge and skills to empower youth for a better future.

Employment

Creating job opportunities and professional development.

Engagement

Encouraging involvement in extracurricular and social activities.

Environment

Promoting sustainability and awareness for a greener future.
